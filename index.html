Iron Ladder Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

In short: Iron Ladder has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your game is saved only on your phone. The game uses Google services to show optional rewarded video ads, to count how the game is played and to report crashes. None of this includes your name, your email address or anything you type into the game.

Who we are

Iron Ladder ("the game") is a mobile role-playing game published on Google Play by Ferrum Faber ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, write to ferrumfabersupport@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your hero, your progress, your items and your settings (language and sound) are saved in the game's private storage on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. This includes the hero name you choose. Using "Start over" in the game's menu deletes the save, and uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored.

What is collected, and by whom

The game uses three Google services. Each one receives some technical data from your device so that it can work.

Service Why What it receives
Google AdMob To show the rewarded videos you choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. Your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, language, and how you interacted with an ad.
Firebase Analytics To understand how the game is played (for example which screens are used, how far players get, and how often crafting succeeds) so it can be improved. An app instance identifier, device model and operating system, country (estimated from your IP address), app version, session times, and gameplay events made only of numbers and the game's own names for its screens, items and dungeons.
Firebase Crashlytics To find and fix crashes. Crash reports: the error, where in the game it happened, the device model, operating system and app version, and an installation identifier.

The game never sends your hero's name, any text you type, your contacts, your photos, your precise location, or your phone number. We do not sell your data.

Google Play also checks, when the game starts, whether a newer version is available, and offers it through Google Play's own update screen. That check is handled by Google Play.

Ads and your choices

Every video ad in the game is optional: it only plays when you tap a button offering a reward, and closing it early simply means no reward. The game shows no other kinds of ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent message the first time it starts, where you can accept or refuse personalised ads. Until you have answered it, the game sends no analytics or crash reports; once you have answered, it sends them as described above. You can change your answer at any time from the game's menu (Privacy settings on the hero screen). If you refuse, you can still watch rewarded videos, but the ads you see will not be personalised.

Wherever you live, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (on most Android phones: Settings, then Google, then Ads).

How long data is kept

Data stored on your phone stays until you start over or uninstall the game. Analytics data is kept by Google for a limited period set in the Firebase project (between 2 and 14 months). Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days. Advertising data is kept according to Google's own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data about you, and to object to its use. Because the game has no accounts, we cannot link the data Google collects to you personally. To exercise your rights, you can:

  • change your ad consent in the game's Privacy settings;
  • reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings;
  • uninstall the game, which stops all collection;
  • contact us at ferrumfabersupport@gmail.com, and we will help as far as we can.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the game and you want their data removed, contact us.
